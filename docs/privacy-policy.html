Digital Speedometer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what data Digital Speedometer ("the app") accesses and how it's used. In short: the app does not run its own servers and does not collect or store your personal data anywhere outside your device. The two things it does touch — your location and advertising — are explained below.

Location data

The app requests access to your device's location (fine and/or coarse) for one purpose only: to calculate your current speed for the on-screen speedometer display. This happens entirely on your device — your location is never transmitted to us, stored by us, logged, or shared with any third party. We have no server to send it to even if we wanted to.

You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings; the app simply won't be able to show a speed reading without it.

Advertising

The app's home screen shows banner ads served by Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads SDK), which is how the app is funded and offered for free.

To serve and measure ads, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process data such as your advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location, and may use this data (including across other apps) to show more relevant ads, subject to your device's ad personalization settings. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us directly — we don't receive or store it ourselves. You can review or reset your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Settings (Google Settings → Ads).

For details on how Google collects and uses this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's EU User Consent Policy.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or sign-in — the app doesn't know who you are.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, photos, or any other personal files.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond what's bundled in the Google Mobile Ads SDK above.
  • No selling of data — we have none to sell.

Data stored on your device

Your chosen settings — mirror on/off, KM/H or MPH, and your custom number/background colours — are saved locally on your device (Android's standard app storage) so they're remembered next time you open the app. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at the address below and we'll address it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date above.
